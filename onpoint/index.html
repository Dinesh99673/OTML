FAQ

Do You Have Any
Question Please?

How can I track my shipment?

Our 2026-ready operations feature real-time GPS tracking and digital documentation, giving you full visibility and proactive shipment updates from origin to final delivery.

Do you handle DDP and DDU shipments to the USA?

Yes — we specialise in Door Delivery, DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) and DDU (Delivered Duty Unpaid) shipments to the USA, with full coast-to-coast coverage and a US office (OTML INC) in Tampa, FL.

Can you handle hazardous and project cargo?

Absolutely. We have proven expertise in breakbulk, oversized project cargo, HAZ chemicals, reefer/temperature-controlled goods and specialised handling for pharmaceuticals and engineering equipment.
