Agricultural Commodities Trading

Agricultural commodities trading is an independent business vertical of OTML INC, focused on the purchase, sale, sourcing and trading of agricultural products. We connect organic farmers and producers in India with buyers across the world seeking high-quality organic produce, acting as a trusted trading partner that sources, buys and supplies premium commodities — primarily Soybeans, Soy Meal and Lentils — with consistent quality checks and reliable supply at every stage.

Our Core Organic Commodities

Organic Soybeans

High in protein and used as a vegetarian and lactose-free alternative across many food applications, soybeans have become the most widely cultivated legume across the globe. OTML INC sources and trades premium-grade organic soya seeds from certified farms in India, supplying USA and global buyers with assured quality and food-grade packaging.

Organic Soy Meal

Foods rich in soy protein offer a proven alternative to meat and animal-based products. As demand for healthier lifestyles rises globally, consumption of soy meal has grown steadily. OTML INC trades and supplies organic soy meal with assured quality and reliable availability — ideal for animal feed and food processing industries in the USA and beyond.

Organic Lentils

A staple legume in North American, Middle Eastern and South Asian diets, organic lentils are a nutrient-dense, high-fibre protein source with growing demand in health food markets worldwide. OTML INC sources, trades and supplies certified organic lentils in food-grade packaging with certificate of origin and thorough quality inspection.

Our Agri-Commodity Trading Services

Sourcing & Procurement

Direct sourcing from certified organic farms and producers in India. We secure consistent supply, negotiate competitive pricing and aggregate volumes to meet buyer requirements across global markets.

Quality Assurance & Grading

Uncompromising quality checks at every stage — grading, moisture control and inspection to ensure every consignment meets the agreed specifications and international food-grade standards.

Global Trade & Supply

Buying and selling of agricultural commodities across international markets — supplying buyers in the USA, Europe, Middle East and Asia-Pacific with reliable volumes, competitive terms and dependable delivery schedules.
